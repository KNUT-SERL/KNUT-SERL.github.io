About SER Lab

The Soft Electronics & Robotics Lab. at Korea National University of Transportation combines electronics, mechanical engineering, and materials science to create skin-adhesive patches, soft grippers, and wearable devices, while advancing the standardization of tactile sensors. We provide motivated students with personalized guidance, hands-on training with extensive research instruments, and a collaborative, dynamic environment for growth.

Four directions, one goal: electronics and robots that work naturally with people.
